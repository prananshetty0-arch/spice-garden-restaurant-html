About Us

Our Story

Spice Garden was founded in 2005 with a simple dream of serving delicious food and bringing people together. We started our journey as a small family restaurant.

Our restaurant is known for its traditional recipes, fresh ingredients and wonderful flavours. We believe that good food creates beautiful memories.

Today, Spice Garden continues to serve customers with love, quality and dedication. Our aim is to provide every guest with a memorable dining experience.

Our Milestones

  • 2005 - Opened our first restaurant branch.
  • 2010 - Introduced our special family recipes.
  • 2015 - Received an award for excellent food quality.
  • 2020 - Expanded our restaurant services.
